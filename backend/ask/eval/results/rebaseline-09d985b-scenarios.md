**[1] Ambient Scribe — Post-GA Refinements** — Overview · `finding:ambient-scribe-post-ga-refinements`

> Session lock during active dictation currently reads as silent data loss, even though it isn't. The locked state's interaction with an in-progress recording was flagged as unresolved in encounter-view 's own notes, sourced from a May 2025 SSO/MFA interview side comment. Direct observation confirms the practical impact: all 4 shadowed clinicians assumed a locked session had destroyed their draft and restarted dictation from scratch, even though the draft is preserved server-side. IT Security confirmed the 10-minute idle-timeout policy itself is fixed, but an explicit "paused, draft preserved" state is a UI-only fix, not a policy change.

**[2] Interview — Nurse Practitioner on Session Lock and Shared Exam-Room Workstations** — Objective · `raw:2026-04-28-interview-nurse-practitioner-session-lock-shared-workstations`

> The 2026-02-17 contextual inquiry found that a session lock during active dictation freezes the ambient-scribe widget with no message, and that all 4 shadowed clinicians assumed the draft was lost and started dictating again, although the draft survives server-side (2026-02-17-session-lock-during-dictation/session-notes.md:38-41, read with correction-2026-09-27.md in that folder). A recovery flow with an explicit paused, draft-preserved state is designed but has not shipped. That session did not look at exam-room workstations shared by several clinicians. This session asked a primary care nurse practitioner, two months later, what a lock looks like in their visits and how a shared workstation affects it.
